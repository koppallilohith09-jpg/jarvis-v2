Gesture control

On the PC, open Setup → Gestures, allow Windows input if wanted, then choose Arm paired iPad camera. Start the camera here. Hand tracking runs on this iPad; only hand positions go to your PC.

Camera is off.

  • Index finger: move the PC pointer
  • Pinch thumb and index: click once; release before another click
  • Index and middle fingers: scroll up or down
  • Hold a fist: play or pause media once
  • Open palm: pause input

Keep this page visible. Switching apps or losing the connection stops the camera. Use your private HTTPS Tailscale address when away from home.
